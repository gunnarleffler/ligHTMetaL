Built to stay useful

    
Tools for applications that last—without making their maintenance your second job.

    
This suite grew out of a personal app used since 2012. Updating it was a reminder
      that useful software can outlive its dependencies. We wanted tools we could understand,
      self-host, and keep using.

    
    
      
  • Your content stays yours. No telemetry, accounts, or vendor cloud.
    • 
      
  • Useful without ceremony. Ready-built files, sensible defaults, and no runtime dependencies.
    • 
      
  • Independent by design. Use the parts you need, without adopting a framework.
    • 
      
  • Stable by intent. Interface changes cost users time. Treat them that way.
    • 
    

    
The goal is not to do everything. It is to do useful things well, with code small enough
      to maintain. Serious about your data. Less serious about capitalization.

  

  
Everything here runs locally in your browser. Nothing is saved or uploaded automatically.
    Image URLs you insert in the HTML editor can still load remote resources.
